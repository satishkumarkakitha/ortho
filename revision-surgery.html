Revision Surgery (Joint Replacement and Fracture Fixation)

రివిజన్ శస్త్రచికిత్స (కీళ్ల మార్పిడి మరియు ఫ్రాక్చర్ ఫిక్సేషన్)

Repeat surgery for joint replacements or fracture fixations that have loosened, worn out, become infected or failed, planned carefully with specialised implants and bone support.

Eligible for Thursday FREE OP

Symptom Checklist & When to Consult

New or continuing pain after a knee or hip replacement
Feeling of looseness, giving way or instability of the joint
Swelling, redness, warmth or discharge near an old surgical scar
Reduced walking ability or limb shortening after earlier surgery
Implant wear, loosening or breakage seen on X-ray

Treatment Options & Advanced Surgical Techniques

1. Detailed Evaluation:

Clinical examination, X-rays, blood tests and sometimes CT scan or joint fluid tests to find out why the implant is painful or failing, and to check for infection.

2. Revision Joint Replacement:

Loose or worn parts are replaced with specialised revision implants, with augments or bone graft where bone has been lost.

3. Infection Management:

When infection is present, treatment may be done in stages: removal of infected parts, antibiotics, and re-implantation once the infection is controlled.

4. Failed Fracture Fixation:

Broken, loose or non-healed plates and nails are revised with stronger fixation and bone grafting where needed.

Patient Pre-Op Preparation Checklist

  • Bring all previous reports, discharge summaries and implant details if available
  • Blood tests for infection, X-rays, and CT or other scans as advised
  • Control of diabetes, anaemia and other health conditions before surgery

Frequently Asked Questions

Is revision surgery more complex than the first operation?

Usually yes, because of scar tissue, bone loss and special implants. It needs detailed planning and an experienced surgical team.

How do I know my implant needs revision?

Persistent pain, instability, swelling or changes on X-ray are warning signs. Only an examination and tests can confirm whether revision is required.

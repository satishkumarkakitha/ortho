Total Hip Replacement & AVN Management

తొంటి (హిప్) మార్పిడి & AVN నయం చేయడం

Advanced Total Hip Arthroplasty (THA), Bipolar Hemiarthroplasty, and treatment for Avascular Necrosis (AVN) of the femoral head. Restores pain-free leg length and natural hip rotation.

Eligible for Thursday FREE OP

Symptom Checklist & When to Consult

Deep groin pain or buttock pain radiating down to knee
Limping or difficulty putting weight on one leg
Stiffness in hip making it difficult to put on socks or shoes
Pain while sitting or turning in bed
Shortening of affected leg appearance

Treatment Options & Advanced Surgical Techniques

1. Total Hip Arthroplasty (THA):

Replacing damaged hip socket (acetabulum) and femoral head with ceramic/uncemented implants.

2. Core Decompression & PRP for Early AVN:

For Stage 1 & Stage 2 Avascular Necrosis to preserve natural femoral head bone blood supply before collapse.

Can I walk naturally after hip replacement?

Yes, most patients achieve a completely normal walking gait without limping within 4 to 6 weeks.

Symptom Checklist & When to Consult

Pain, swelling or bruising after twisting the ankle or a fall
Ankle that frequently gives way or feels unstable
Painful bump at the base of the big toe (bunion) or flattened arch
Pain at the back of the heel or along the Achilles tendon
Difficulty walking, standing for long or wearing normal footwear

Treatment Options & Advanced Surgical Techniques

1. Non-Surgical Care:

Rest, bracing, supportive footwear or insoles, physiotherapy and medicines are enough for many sprains, flat foot and heel problems.

2. Fracture Fixation:

Ankle and foot fractures that are displaced or unstable are fixed with plates, screws or wires so the joint heals in the correct position.

3. Ligament and Keyhole Procedures:

Recurrent instability or cartilage injuries can be treated with ligament repair or reconstruction and ankle arthroscopy.

4. Corrective Surgery:

Bunion correction, flat foot reconstruction and Achilles procedures are considered when conservative treatment has not helped.

Patient Pre-Op Preparation Checklist

  • X-ray of the foot or ankle (weight-bearing views if advised), MRI when needed
  • Blood tests, ECG and anaesthesia fitness
  • Arrange help at home and a walking aid before surgery

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every ankle sprain need surgery?

No. Most sprains heal with rest, bracing and physiotherapy. Surgery is considered mainly for repeated instability or associated injuries.

When can I walk after foot or ankle surgery?

It depends on the procedure. Many patients walk with protection and limited weight for several weeks; your doctor will give a clear plan after surgery.
